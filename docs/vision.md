# Mission And Vision

## Mission
Provide secure, reliable, and maintainable support for sports organizations, clubs, and friend groups by handling session logistics so people can focus on playtime instead of operations.

## Vision
Deliver a web-first product that can be deployed to mobile with minimal rework, starting with volleyball and expanding to other sports through extensible domain models and workflows.

## Product Scope
- Organize competitions: seasonal leagues, weekly leagues, and single or multi-day tournaments.
- Manage players, groups, and subgroups.
- Track player sports statistics.
- Manage playing spaces and availability, including courts.
- Manage equipment and logistics inventory.
- Support payment collection and related operational workflows.

## Product Principles
- Correctness before convenience for competition logic.
- Security by default for authentication, authorization, and data handling.
- Clarity over cleverness in code and documentation.
- Incremental delivery through small, testable steps.
